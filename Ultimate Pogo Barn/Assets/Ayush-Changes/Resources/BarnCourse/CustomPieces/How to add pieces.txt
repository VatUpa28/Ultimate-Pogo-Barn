Make a prefab from your GameObject and give its root one of these tags.

BarnObstacle adds it to the obstacle choices. Use a collider on the Default layer for a solid surface.
BarnPlatform adds it to the platform choices. Use a flat 3D deck. The game fits it to a 5 by 5 section and adds a box collider if it has no solid collider.

Save the prefab in this folder. Its filename is the name shown in the chooser.
